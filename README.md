# Focuset 🌟

**Focuset** is a lightweight digital learning support platform designed to help self-paced learners stay motivated, consistent, and engaged in their learning journeys.

[🚀 Try the MVP](https://focuset.netlify.app)

---

## 🔹 Product Overview
Focuset is **not a content platform**—it's a learning companion. By integrating into existing study routines, it helps learners:
- Set daily goals  
- Track progress visually  
- Maintain streaks  
- Receive reminders for accountability  
- Earn small rewards to reinforce consistent habits  

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Self-paced learners often struggle with:
- Motivation and consistency  
- Procrastination and overwhelm  
- Lack of accountability and emotional support  

Existing platforms focus on content delivery, leaving learners to self-regulate—often leading to course abandonment.

---

## 💡 Solution
Focuset addresses these challenges with **behavioral and emotional support**:
- **Goal Setting:** Break big goals into achievable tasks  
- **Progress Visualization:** See growth and milestones clearly  
- **Reminder System:** Gentle nudges to stay on track  
- **Streak Tracker & Rewards:** Build motivation through positive reinforcement  

---

## 👥 Target Users
### Timi – Student Learner
> "I have big academic goals but without structure, I lose momentum."

### Mrs. Ada – Career Professional
> "I want to grow professionally but after work, motivation is my biggest battle."

Focuset also supports independent learners juggling multiple courses or skills.

---

## 📈 Key Features (MVP)
- **Set Daily Goals:** Create, edit, and track goals  
- **Reminders & Check-ins:** Gentle, timely notifications  
- **Streak Tracker:** Visualize consecutive learning days  
- **Rewards:** Earn badges and celebrate milestones  
- **Dashboard Home:** Central view of all progress and tasks  

---

## 🛠 Key Learnings & Iteration
1. **Simplification Drives Adoption**  
   - Reducing onboarding steps increased completion by 35%  
   - *Action:* Implemented progressive disclosure in the setup wizard  

2. **Pain-Point Prioritization**  
   - Features addressing top HR/learner frustrations saw 3x higher usage  
   - *Action:* Re-prioritized roadmap based on user pain scores  

3. **Wireframes as Validation Tools**  
   - Early wireframes identified critical UX improvements before development  
   - *Action:* Instituted wireframe testing as a development gate
